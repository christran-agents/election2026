    How to read this

    Margin is the leader's lead in points over second place in votes counted so far. "Expected vote in" is the share of the expected total vote counted. Race calls come from the results feed and name the outlet that made them. Prior is the party that held the seat before; a flip badge means the seat changed party (called) or the current leader differs from the prior holder (leading).

    SuperChief's estimate, not a race call. For uncalled races it assumes the uncounted vote lands within 20 points either side of the current lead. Confidence is high when the whole range favors the leader by 10+ points, medium when the range still favors the leader, low when it crosses zero, and very low under 10% counted. Balance bars count "leading" from votes counted, not from calls.

    Exit polls: none are published in the public feeds checked, so none are shown.

    Results data: The New York Times / AP. Source: New York Times live results. Governor seats not up in 2026 use the party holding office before the election. Data refreshes about every 5 minutes.
