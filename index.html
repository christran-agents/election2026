How the estimate works

SuperChief's estimate is not a race call. For each uncalled race it takes the current lead and assumes the uncounted vote lands within 20 points either side of it, so the range is the current margin plus or minus 20 points times the share still uncounted. Confidence is high when the whole range favors the leader and the lead is 10 points or more, medium when the range still favors the leader, low when it crosses zero, and very low under 10% reported.

A called flip comes from the results feed. A possible flip means the current leader's party differs from the incumbent's. Competitive House combines the feed's toss-up and lean ratings with the closest uncalled margins, up to 60 races. Too close to call lists uncalled races with a lead under 5 points and low or very low confidence.
